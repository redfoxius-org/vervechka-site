Three steps: in Q4 2026 we build the MVP, in Q1 2027 we look for pilot schools and start the pilot, and in Q2–Q4 2027 we bring the product to its best based on the results of the pilots with schools.

The quarters are **plans, not promises**: dates may slip, and the scope will be refined by school feedback. The label next to each capability shows what is done, what is in development and what is only planned. No school uses the platform yet.

This page mirrors the product's development plan; when the plan changes, so does this page.
