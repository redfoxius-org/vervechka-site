These are not screenshots but **illustrations of the interface design**: we rebuilt the product's mockups as part of this page. All names, phone numbers and titles are invented, and so is "Demo School". No real children, parents or staff appear here.

Every screen carries a status: "Available now", "In development" or "Planned", the same as on the [What is ready](/en/roadmap/) page. Even on available screens some of what the design shows is not built yet; the caption says which parts.

The interface always shows the **school's** colors and logo, not Vervechka's: parents and children see their own school. The illustrations use a neutral blue from the set a school can choose from.
