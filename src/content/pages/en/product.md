Vervechka has three parts: the **school server** (a separate one for every school), the **staff web cabinet** for administrators and teachers, and the **mobile app for parents** on iOS and Android. The product is in development: the capabilities below are the MVP we are building.

Below are the capabilities by area. The timing by quarter is on the [Roadmap](/en/roadmap/) page.

The interface is bilingual (Ukrainian and English). Legal documents (the Certificate of Achievement, invoices and statements) are in Ukrainian only.
